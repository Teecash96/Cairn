Cairn FAQ

Short answers about the planner, privacy, timing, wallet identity, and sharing.

What is Cairn?

Cairn turns a rough product idea into an editable product brief, user flow, builder pack, and reality check.

What leaves my phone?

The description you submit is sent through Cairn's server to Google Gemini. Your saved plans stay in local storage unless you choose to share one.

How long does a plan take?

Most plans are ready in under 30 seconds. Network conditions and model load can change the time.

Can I share a plan?

Yes. Sharing is an explicit action. A shared link is read only, does not expose private tracker notes, and can be revoked by its creator.
