Cairn privacy policy

This page explains what the mini app sends, stores, and shares. Last updated 6 September 2026.

What stays on your device

Your saved plans and private Track data are stored in your browser's local storage. Cairn does not require an email account, password, or contact list. A protected team workspace is optional and uses a short lived signed Nimiq wallet session.

What is sent for generation

When you generate or refine a plan, the text you submit is sent to Cairn's Cloudflare Worker. The Worker sends the request to Google Gemini to produce the response. Cairn does not put an API key in the app.

Wallet information

Cairn receives the connected Nimiq wallet address to verify your identity, protect team access, and assign paid AI credits. Your wallet signs a short lived login challenge. Cairn does not receive or store your private key. NIM payments go from your wallet to Cairn's receiving address. The Worker checks the network transaction before it grants credits.

Refinement and network limits

A planner follow up sends the PRD, flow, and text only builder pack to Cairn's Worker and Google Gemini for that one response. Private tracker notes, labels, dates, priorities, status, and dependencies stay on your device. Cairn uses a short lived, sanitized network address during wallet sign in and abuse checks. It is not used for analytics or advertising.

Sharing

Nothing is shared until you tap Share. A public shared plan is uploaded to the Worker so another person can open its read only link. The snapshot includes progress, milestone dates, task due dates, and labels. It excludes notes, priorities, and dependency details. Do not share confidential material.

Protected team workspaces

The owner can create an optional protected workspace for Track and add named Nimiq wallet addresses as Viewers or Editors. Members must sign in with the wallet that the owner added. The Worker checks the wallet session and role on every request. The team snapshot includes milestone and task text, progress, labels, dates, and the owner's milestone blocker flag. It excludes the PRD, user flow, Build summary, private notes, priorities, and dependency details. Team records remain available for up to one year unless removed sooner.

Tracking and analytics

Cairn has no Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or third party tracking script. This keeps the mini app small and matches its privacy promise.

Security and retention

Cloudflare manages encryption at rest for server records. Wallet sessions expire after twelve hours and login challenges expire after five minutes. Public shared plans and protected team records remain available for up to one year, unless the storage provider removes them sooner. Do not put confidential material in a shared plan or team tracker.

Questions

For a privacy question or a correction request, open an issue in the public Cairn repository. Do not include private wallet keys or confidential product documents.
