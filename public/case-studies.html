Case studies and examples

See the route from idea to release.

These are illustrative examples, not customer claims. They show how Cairn turns a short description into focused work and evidence.

Cairn does not invent market research or performance results. Validate every assumption with real users before you build.

Example 01

Voice to shopping list

A rough idea about turning a voice note into a shopping list becomes a brief for the shopper, a six step flow, and a small first release.

  • Core path: record, transcribe, group, check, shop
  • Early test: compare the list with one real basket
Example 02

Pothole reporting

A civic reporting concept becomes a plan that separates resident input from the council review queue.

  • Core path: locate, photograph, submit, triage
  • Early test: ask one council worker to rank five reports
Example 03

Fast freelance invoices

A freelancer's frustration becomes a focused workflow with a clear outcome and explicit out of scope list.

  • Core path: choose client, add work, review, send
  • Early test: time one invoice from start to send
